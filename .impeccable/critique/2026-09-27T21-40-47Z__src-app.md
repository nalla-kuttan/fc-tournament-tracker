## Anti-Patterns Verdict
LLM: Home, tournament and match entry now read as owned. Remaining generic tells sit in the deeper Stats pages: "What matters now" / "Control Room Read" kickers, 10.4px all-caps award nicknames ("Iron Wall", "MOTM Magnet"), "Global Analytics" as a title.
Detector: CLI 0 (does not read MUI sx). Browser overlays: tournament @375 8, Player rankings @375 20, home @1440 15, Stats summary @1440 4 (tournament page was 27 in the first run). Real: letter avatars for players without photos, dark text on #757575 at 4.3:1; layout-property animation hits are MUI Accordion/Collapse. False positives: "single font" and "dark mode with glowing accents" (required by DESIGN.md), "cramped padding" on MUI Chips. One white-on-coral hit on home could not be located on the settled page (likely a transient hover/transition state).

## Priority Issues
- [P1] Stats pages don't match their navigation and keep the old voice: titles differ from nav labels, top-level pages have "Back" links, copy says "analytics lens", "match intelligence", "competitive signals", "Min MP". Command: /impeccable clarify
- [P1] Stats sub-navigation hides three of six destinations on phones (Tournaments, Competition history, Ask AI), and Competition history cuts off "Trophy Cabinet"; same fix pattern as the tournament tabs. Command: /impeccable adapt
- [P2] Player profile is 5,521px tall on a phone, repeats headline numbers in "Career Stats", and marks stat numbers up as h4/h6 headings, so the screen-reader outline is a list of numbers. Command: /impeccable distill
- [P2] Rivalries opens as two empty pickers and a disabled Compare button even though Summary already knows the busiest rivalry (Alex vs Ruban). Command: /impeccable onboard

## Persona Red Flags
- Casey (phone): can't see Tournaments, Competition history or Ask AI in the Stats nav without scrolling it; the player profile is five-plus screens of scrolling.
- Sam (screen reader): player profile heading outline is numbers ("60%", "309", "3.36"); letter avatars at 4.3:1.
- Jordan (first visit to Stats): "Min MP", "analytics lens", "match intelligence" need translation; Rivalries starts blank.
- Match-night admin: now well served (photo read, save & next, season restart).

## Minor Observations
- "Record book" on home opens Competition history on the Season tab rather than Records.
- Player pages hard-code their own W/D/L colors (draw is #C9B9BE there, #A18A93 elsewhere) instead of FORM_COLORS.
- Stats shows 1,271 goals (scorelines) vs 1,272 goal records because of the Basil 1-0 Arshad record.
