## Anti-Patterns Verdict
LLM: identity (bordeaux/coral, Chakra Petch) is owned and mostly on-brief; slop tells are local: gradient-text title, "TOURNAMENT COMMAND"/"RECORDS & MILESTONES" eyebrows, identical emoji record cards.
Detector: CLI scan of src/app + src/components found 0 (does not read MUI sx). Browser overlays: tournament page (mobile) 27, home 19, stats 5. Real: form-badge contrast 2.9:1 (W on #EA6C56), 3.6:1 (L on #EF4444), 3.0:1 (D on #A18A93); gradient-text on tournament h4 (src/app/tournaments/[tournamentId]/layout.tsx:57); hero eyebrow chip; clipped Tabs; layout-property animation and long line length on stats. False positives: "single font" and "dark mode with glowing accents" (DESIGN.md mandates both), most "cramped padding" (MUI Chip internals), one 1.0:1 contrast (layered same-colour text).

## Priority Issues
- [P1] Result entry is slow for how the group actually uses it. ~15 fields per match live in two collapsed accordions with bare number fields and sliders; after saving it returns to the dashboard instead of the next fixture; score inputs have no accessible name (label is an unlinked Typography, MatchResultForm.tsx:249-273). Fix: one open two-column home/away stat sheet in the in-game stats order, +/- steppers on score and counts, "Save & next fixture". Command: /impeccable shape
- [P1] Home misreports state after a season ends (page.tsx:414 falls back to latest tournament; copy at :301 says "active" unconditionally) and offers no "Start next season". Fix: completed state shows champion + "Start Season 25" as primary. Command: /impeccable clarify
- [P1] Mobile standings hide Pts, GD and Form (table 768px in a 375px viewport); only Dashboard/Matches tabs visible. Fix: phone layout #/Player/P/GD/Pts/Form, team under name; full-width tab bar or segmented control. Command: /impeccable adapt
- [P2] Contrast and system violations: form badges below 4.5:1, gradient title, eyebrow chips. Command: /impeccable polish
- [P2] Home is a long stack of equal-weight panels (tournaments, records carousel, power table, hall of fame, recent results) with no clear second priority after the hero. Command: /impeccable distill

## Persona Red Flags
- Casey (phone on match night): score is typed into number fields, stats hidden behind two accordions, Pts off-screen in standings, "Standings" tab hidden behind a chevron.
- Alex (organizer entering 10 results): every save bounces to the dashboard; no keyboard stepping; re-opening each accordion per match.
- Sam (screen reader / low vision): unnamed score inputs; W/D/L badges at 2.9-3.6:1.
- Project persona, "Match-night admin" (controller in one hand, phone in the other): needs one-thumb score entry and an obvious "next fixture" after each save.

## Minor Observations
- Recent results panel on the tournament dashboard truncates names ("Moa…") and the tag chip overlaps "Manchester City" at 1440px.
- Match pages highlight the Dashboard tab instead of Matches.
- "0 fixtures pending" is the headline of a completed tournament; show the champion/final table instead.
- Tournament pages load with a centered spinner; use skeletons.
- Home stat strip shows 1,272 goals (goal rows) while Stats shows 1,271 (scorelines) because of one bad record.
- Recent results rows don't emphasize the winner.

## Questions to Consider
- What if entering a result took one screen and one thumb, then dropped you on the next fixture?
- What should the app say the moment a season ends: the champion, or the next season?
- Which three numbers must be visible on a phone without scrolling sideways?
