# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FC Tournament Tracker is for friend groups running EA SPORTS FC/FIFA tournament nights. Users are players and organizers who need to create tournaments, generate schedules or brackets, enter match results, review standings, compare rivalries, and relive the story of the competition through player profiles, analytics, and AI-written summaries.

## Product Purpose

The product turns casual FC nights into a durable competitive record. It should make match administration fast enough to use during play, make standings and brackets trustworthy at a glance, and make accumulated stats feel personal, memorable, and worth coming back to after the session.

## Brand Personality

Competitive, cinematic, precise. The interface should feel like a serious match-night control room for a close friend group: energetic and dramatic without losing clarity, confident in the data, and specific enough that it never feels like a generic dashboard template.

## Anti-references

Do not let the app feel generic. Avoid anonymous SaaS dashboard patterns, bland chart panels, interchangeable card grids, and decorative esports styling that does not help users understand tournaments, rivalries, form, or match state.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next tournament action within reach, whether that is creating a bracket, entering a result, checking standings, or opening a player profile.
2. Make competitive status instantly legible through hierarchy, ranking, form, and clear state language.
3. Treat stats as storytelling material, not just table cells; rivalry, momentum, awards, and AI summaries should make the history feel alive.
4. Preserve match-night energy while keeping controls, forms, and tables familiar enough to trust under pressure.
5. Give every surface a specific FC Tracker point of view so the product feels owned, not skinned.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA as the practical baseline for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard navigation, responsive behavior, and readable text. Respect reduced-motion preferences, especially around glow, pulse, animated section reveals, and celebratory effects. Avoid communicating status by color alone; pair color with labels, icons, ordering, or copy.
